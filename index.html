🧠 THE SCIENCE

Why Does Sunrise & Sunset Happen?

The answer is Earth's rotation.

01
🌍

Earth Rotates

Earth continuously rotates around its own axis.

02
☀️

Sunlight Reaches Earth

The side of Earth facing the Sun receives sunlight.

03
🌅

Sunrise

As a location rotates into sunlight, sunrise occurs.

04
🌇

Sunset

As a location rotates away from sunlight, sunset occurs.

🌎 In Simple Words

The Sun appears to rise in the east and set in the west because Earth rotates from west to east. When your location rotates into sunlight, you experience sunrise. When it rotates away from sunlight, you experience sunset.

📚 PROJECT NOTES

Notes About Sunrise & Sunset

Simple scientific notes to understand how Earth, the Sun and rotation create day and night.

🌍

Earth's Rotation

Earth continuously rotates around its own axis. One complete rotation takes approximately 24 hours.

☀️

Sunlight

The Sun provides the light that illuminates Earth. At any moment, approximately one half of Earth is facing the Sun.

🌅

Why Sunrise Happens

As Earth rotates, a location moves from the dark side toward the Sun-facing side. This creates sunrise.

🌇

Why Sunset Happens

As Earth continues rotating, a location moves away from the Sun-facing side. This creates sunset.

🧭

Earth Rotates West to East

Earth rotates from west to east. This rotation is why the Sun appears to rise in the east and set in the west.

📍

Location Matters

Sunrise and sunset times are different for different locations because latitude, longitude and date affect the Sun's position.

📅

Date Matters

Sunrise and sunset times change throughout the year because Earth's axis is tilted and Earth moves around the Sun.
